## Resolve AI Product Deep Dive: Your New Autonomous Teammate

## Resolve AI 产品深入剖析：你的新自主队友

Engineers often find themselves bogged down with operational tasks like incident response, leaving little time for what they love most – coding and building new things. On-call duties are particularly draining, constantly interrupting workflows and adding stress to the day. That’s why we’re thrilled to introduce our first product, [**AI Production Engineer**](https://resolve.ai/product/overview) – a tireless, autonomous teammate designed to handle alerts, perform [root cause analysis](https://resolve.ai/glossary/what-is-root-cause-analysis), resolve incidents, and make on-call stress-free.

工程师常常被事故响应这类运维工作拖住，留给最热爱的事情——写代码和构建新东西——的时间少得可怜。值班尤其消耗精力，它不断打断工作节奏，给一天增添压力。因此我们非常兴奋地推出第一款产品 [**AI 生产工程师**](https://resolve.ai/product/overview)——一位不知疲倦的自主队友，专为处理告警、执行[根因分析](https://resolve.ai/glossary/what-is-root-cause-analysis)、解决事故而设计，让值班不再有压力。

Production incidents are high-stakes events. They can disrupt customer experiences, affect revenue, and even put your company’s reputation at risk. The unpredictability of these issues, combined with limited visibility and the challenge of coordinating across teams, makes them exhausting to deal with – and often leads to burnout. But it doesn’t have to be this way.

生产事故是高风险事件。它们会破坏客户体验、影响营收，甚至让公司的声誉面临风险。这类问题本身不可预测，加上可见性有限、跨团队协调困难，处理起来令人疲惫，而且往往导致职业倦怠。但事情不必如此。

### Meet Your New On-Call Teammate

### 认识你的新值班队友

Resolve AI has built a foundational AI platform for production engineering that transforms incident investigation and resolution. This allows engineers to stay focused on what they do best – innovating and shaping the future. Resolve AI understands your [production systems](https://resolve.ai/glossary/what-are-production-systems-in-software-engineering) and tools, responds to alerts, collaborates with you, and saves you time by identifying what’s wrong, how to fix it, and building an automated post-incident review.

Resolve AI 构建了一个面向生产工程的基础性 AI 平台，彻底改变了事故调查与解决的方式。这让工程师可以专注于自己最擅长的事——创新并塑造未来。Resolve AI 理解你的[生产系统](https://resolve.ai/glossary/what-are-production-systems-in-software-engineering)和工具，响应告警，与你协作，并通过指出问题所在、如何修复以及生成自动化的事后复盘来为你节省时间。

### Understands Your Systems and Tools at Light-speed

### 以光速理解你的系统与工具

Getting started with Resolve AI is quick. It seamlessly connects with all your infrastructure – right down to individual pods, and to your tools from observability platforms like Grafana and Datadog, to [CI/CD pipelines](https://resolve.ai/glossary/what-is-the-future-of-CI_CD) like Jenkins and codebase in GitHub.

上手 Resolve AI 很快。它能无缝连接你的全部基础设施——小到单个 pod，也连接你的各种工具，从 Grafana、Datadog 等可观测性平台，到 Jenkins 这类 [CI/CD 流水线](https://resolve.ai/glossary/what-is-the-future-of-CI_CD)，以及 GitHub 中的代码库。

From the moment you integrate Resolve AI, it begins comprehensively mapping your environment and constructs a dynamic knowledge graph of your entire system and tools, continuously updating it in real-time as new deployments, system events, configuration changes, or code changes occur. This deep, up-to-the-minute understanding empowers Resolve AI to traverse all dependencies, pods, and deployments swiftly, and it responds accurately whenever you need it most.

从你集成 Resolve AI 的那一刻起，它就开始全面绘制你的环境，并构建整个系统与工具的动态知识图谱，在出现新部署、系统事件、配置变更或代码变更时实时持续更新。这种深入且实时的理解，让 Resolve AI 能迅速遍历所有依赖、pod 和部署，并在你最需要的时候准确响应。

### Responds to Alerts Before You Log In

### 在你登录之前就响应告警

When an alert is triggered, Resolve AI springs into action. Like an on-call engineer, it immediately begins investigating by examining all the relevant data. It autonomously creates and executes a set of just-in-time runbooks, reviewing metrics, dashboards, code changes, deployments, and logs.

告警触发时，Resolve AI 立即行动。它与值班工程师一样，马上开始检查所有相关数据展开调查。它自主创建并执行一组即时生成的运维手册，查看指标、仪表板、代码变更、部署和日志。

In less than a minute, Resolve AI has already pinpointed a root cause theory and suggested steps to fix it. You get a clear head start, without the usual chaos.

不到一分钟，Resolve AI 就已经确定了根因推测，并给出修复步骤。你能清楚地抢占先机，而不必经历通常的混乱。

### Tells You What’s Wrong and How to Fix It

### 告诉你哪里出了问题以及如何修复

Resolve AI conducts a comprehensive analysis of the entire event, tracking every change and system behavior to pinpoint the root cause. It can intelligently interpret dashboards, review logs, and detect anomalies. Whether it's a configuration error, code change, downstream service issue, or deployment problem, Resolve AI identifies it all with precision.

Resolve AI 会对整个事件做全面分析，追踪每一处变更和系统行为，从而定位根因。它能智能地解读仪表板、查看日志并检测异常。无论是配置错误、代码变更、下游服务问题，还是部署问题，Resolve AI 都能精准识别。

It develops a theory, explains how it arrived there, and provides actionable steps to resolve it. It applies complex human-like logic, judgment and reasoning at every step of the way. No more guessing games or digging through tools for hours.

它会形成推测，解释自己是如何得出该推测的，并给出可操作的解决步骤。它在每一步都运用类似人类的复杂逻辑、判断与推理。不必再靠猜，也不必再在工具里翻找几个小时。

### Collaborate, Guide, or Let Resolve AI Take the Wheel

### 与它协作、给它指导，或让 Resolve AI 接手

You can work with Resolve AI as if it were a teammate. Ask it questions, explore other theories, or even tell it to take action, like rolling back to the last deployment or restarting a pod. You can even loop it into your conversations with @mentions in Slack or through the Resolve AI UI.

你可以把 Resolve AI 当作队友一样配合。向它提问、探索其他推测，甚至让它执行操作，例如回滚到上一次部署或重启 pod。你还可以在 Slack 里用 @mention 或通过 Resolve AI UI 把它拉进你们的对话。

### Saves You Time on Incident Reviews

### 在事故复盘上为你节省时间

Once the incident is resolved, Resolve AI puts together a detailed post-incident review, summarizing everything from the initial alert to the root cause and the steps taken to resolve it. This saves you hours of operational time and gives you a clear, concise record to learn from and improve for next time.

一旦事故解决，Resolve AI 会整理出一份详细的事后复盘，总结从最初告警到根因以及所采取解决步骤的全部内容。这为你省下数小时的运维时间，并给出一份清晰、简洁的记录，供你学习并在下次改进。
